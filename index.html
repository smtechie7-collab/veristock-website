    Get answers to common queries regarding VeriStock Pro setup, security, and usage.

    Offline-First means the app performs all calculations, database updates, stock adjustments, and invoice generations directly on your device. You do not require a working internet connection to manage inventory, record customer payments, or search billing logs. It prevents sales disruption during connection blackouts.
    Absolutely. VeriStock Pro encrypts the local database at rest using military-grade SQLCipher (256-bit AES). No cloud service has read access to your inventory values, customer balances, or revenue records unless you manually export them.
    Yes, VeriStock Pro fully complies with Indian GST requirements. It supports CGST, SGST, IGST tax structures, HSN code registration for products, itemized discount margins, and outputs structured GST CSV files for simplified tax returns filing.
    You can easily back up your encrypted data. VeriStock Pro integrates a Google Drive Sync tool. Backups are encrypted before transmission using your private credentials and written to your personal Google Drive cloud workspace.
    Repair Job Sheets are digital tickets for tracking repair workflows. They record item details, lock patterns, estimated parts cost, diagnostics notes, technician assignments, and repair status stages.
    The app supports all standard 2-inch (58mm) and 3-inch (80mm) ESC/POS thermal printers over Bluetooth. You can print invoices, repair intake receipts, and barcodes wirelessly.
    You can trigger account deletion directly in the app via Settings → About App → Delete Account, or via our website's Account Deletion Page. This permanently deletes your cloud auth profile, session tokens, and linked metadata within 7 business days. Your local device database remains and is removed only when you uninstall the app.
    Yes, designed for mobile stores, you can register unique IMEI values or device serial codes for items and search for them instantly during billing or warranty validation.
    Yes, the app accesses your device's Camera to scan and identify product barcodes instantly. It also supports external physical USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners.
    Uninstalling the Android app will erase all app-specific private folders, including the local database, unless you have backed it up to Google Drive or exported CSV logs. We recommend performing a backup before uninstalling.
    Currently, VeriStock Pro is optimized exclusively for Android mobile and tablet devices. Desktop versions are planned in our long-term roadmap.
    Yes, the app features a customer ledger. You can record payments, outstanding balances, and send reminder details, keeping account relationships transparent.
    You can export daily, weekly, or custom range sales, stock, and ledger books directly as CSV files. These can be shared via email or messaging apps for compliance auditing.
    Yes, the app allows uploading your business name, mobile, address, GSTIN, and custom logo image to print at the header of your receipts automatically.
    VeriStock Pro requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or above. It needs at least 2GB of RAM and 100MB of free storage space. Barcode scanning requires a camera with auto-focus.
    VeriStock Pro runs local database sessions on each device. For multi-staff operations, we offer two options: (1) Smart Clone — a one-time local device-to-device data transfer that works by placing two phones in close proximity using the Google Nearby API (no internet required, no real-time sync); and (2) Google Drive Backup — the owner device creates an encrypted backup which staff devices can restore from. Note: Smart Clone is not a live multi-device sync; it is a one-time migration tool.
