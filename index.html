    Frequently Asked Questions

    Get answers to common queries regarding VeriStock Pro setup, security, and usage.

    Hybrid Offline-First means the app performs all calculations, database updates, stock adjustments, and invoice generations directly on your device with 100% operational autonomy — even during internet blackouts. When connected to the internet, all transactions and inventory changes automatically synchronize in real-time to Google Cloud & Firebase Firestore, keeping all staff devices, branch counters, and your Command Centre dashboard perfectly aligned.
    Absolutely. VeriStock Pro encrypts the local database at rest using military-grade SQLCipher (256-bit AES). When synchronizing with Google Cloud & Firebase Firestore, data is encrypted in transit over TLS 1.3 and stored under strictly isolated multi-tenant security rules (businesses/{bizId}/shops/{shopId}/*). There is zero commercial telemetry or third-party ad tracking.
    Yes, VeriStock Pro fully complies with Indian GST requirements. It supports CGST, SGST, IGST tax structures, HSN code registration for products, itemized discount margins, and outputs structured GST CSV files for simplified tax returns filing.
    VeriStock Pro uses an enterprise Write-Ahead Outbox engine backed by WorkManager. Every sale, purchase, expense, and stock change is safely committed locally first, then synced automatically in the background to Google Cloud & Firebase Firestore. In addition, you can export full encrypted database snapshots and CSV accounting reports on demand.
    Repair Job Sheets are digital tickets for tracking repair workflows. They record item details, lock patterns, estimated parts cost, diagnostics notes, technician assignments, and repair status stages with live multi-device synchronization across your technicians.
    The app supports all standard 2-inch (58mm) and 3-inch (80mm) ESC/POS thermal printers over Bluetooth. You can print invoices, repair intake receipts, and barcodes wirelessly.
    You can trigger account deletion directly in the app via Settings → About App → Delete Account, or via our website's Account Deletion Page. This permanently deletes your Firebase Auth profile, cloud session tokens, and linked cloud business records within 7 business days. Your local device database is removed when you uninstall the app.
    Yes, designed for mobile stores, you can register unique IMEI values or device serial codes for items and search for them instantly during billing or warranty validation.
    Yes, the app accesses your device's Camera to scan and identify product barcodes instantly. It also supports external physical USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners.
    Uninstalling the Android app will erase local app-specific private folders and SQLCipher database on that handset. However, your synced cloud records in Firestore remain safe and can be restored immediately upon logging into your Firebase account on any new device.
    Yes! VeriStock Pro includes a dedicated, secure CA Web Portal. Chartered Accountants and auditors can log in with their authorized credentials to review real-time P&L summaries, balance ledgers, and download ready-to-file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B tax reports.
    Yes, the app features a customer ledger. You can record payments, outstanding balances, and send reminder details, keeping account relationships transparent across all branches.
    You can export daily, weekly, or custom range sales, stock, and ledger books directly as CSV files from the Android app, or your Chartered Accountant can access them directly via the real-time CA Web Portal.
    Yes, the app allows uploading your business name, mobile, address, GSTIN, and custom logo image to print at the header of your receipts automatically.
    VeriStock Pro features a built-in Owner Control Tower with a live OpenStreetMap & Leaflet fleet radar. Business owners and managers can define shop geofence perimeters and see real-time color-coded pulsing pins for staff (Green = In Store, Blue = On Field, Red = Geofence Breach) with 15-minute duty tracking. The system automatically tracks shift grace times, 4-way breaks (Lunch/Tea/Prayer/Personal), flags mock GPS tampering, and records complete 30-day visual attendance heatmaps.
    Yes! The Staff Advance Wallet allows owners to disburse salary advances with automatic double-entry ledger debiting (Article 4 compliant). Monthly payroll automatically factors in base salary, multi-tier overtime rates (Weekday 1.0x, Sunday 1.5x, Holiday 2.0x), real-time sales & repair labor commissions, and auto-deducts outstanding advances. Owners can generate professional A4 PDF payslips with SHA-256 integrity verification and share them with staff directly over WhatsApp in 1 click.
    VeriStock Pro requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or above. It needs at least 2GB of RAM and 100MB of free storage space. Barcode scanning requires a camera with auto-focus.
    Yes! VeriStock Pro features a complete Multi-User & Staff Management architecture with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Business owners can invite Managers, Salesmen, Technicians, Accountants, and CAs via Firebase email invites. Each staff member logs into their own device and only sees the features and data permitted for their role (e.g. salesmen cannot see purchase cost prices, and technicians only see assigned repair tickets). All transactions sync across all store devices in real-time.
    No. VeriStock Pro is a premium B2B utility. It is completely ad-free. Your screen workspace will never show promotional banner ads.
    Yes, our email and remote support coordinates handle inquiries in English, Hindi, and Gujarati. Write to us at smtechie7@gmail.com for configuration help.
    Basic inventory, invoice logs, and printer setups are free. Advanced feature bundles require license activation keys, distributed via SM Technologies channel partners.
