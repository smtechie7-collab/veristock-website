The Modern 4-Step Clinical Workflow

Designed for fast-paced OPD centers where every second counts and zero patient confusion is tolerated.

1

Patient Intake

Receptionist registers patient name, age, gender, mobile number, and issues sequential daily Token #.

2

Vitals Log

Nursing station measures and enters Blood Pressure, Pulse, SpO2, and Weight directly onto the patient card.

3

Doctor Consultation

Doctor reviews vitals history, performs examination, and builds standardized digital Rx in seconds.

4

Print & Fee Collect

Print A4/Thermal Rx, collect consultation fee via Cash/UPI, and hand over to patient or in-house pharmacy.
