Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to join VeriStock Connect?

Any registered merchant with an active business setup on VeriStock Pro can apply. Access requires completing mandatory 5-gate eligibility: verified mobile number, active shop profile, valid GSTIN/PAN/Aadhaar documents, geotagged storefront photo, and DPDP Act 2023 legal consent.

Does VeriStock charge a commission on Connect sales?

VeriStock Pro does not charge unfair marketplace listing fees or middleman commissions on peer discovery or direct counter transactions. Standard escrow payment gateway processing charges apply only when choosing Razorpay Escrow protection.

How is location spoofing or fake reviews prevented?

Our native Android engine includes an Anti-Mock GPS Guard that detects developer option location spoofing and blocks access immediately. Reviews and Trust Scores can only be generated after an authentic delivery OTP is validated on both ends.
