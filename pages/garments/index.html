Garments FAQ

When you add a garment to an order, a grid popup shows all available size-color combinations with current stock counts. You select the exact variant the customer wants and quantity. The billing system deducts precisely that size and color from inventory — no manual adjustment needed.
When a customer returns an item for exchange, you create a Credit Note for the return value. The returned item goes back in stock. On the new purchase, you can apply the credit note as partial payment. If the new item is cheaper, the remaining credit balance stays on the customer account for future purchases.
A Job Work Challan is a document issued when you send fabric or semi-finished garments to an external vendor (tailor, embroiderer, dyer) for processing. VeriStock Pro creates a digital challan recording: items sent, quantity, vendor name, expected return date, and processing instructions. When goods return, you match against the challan and update stock.
Garments with MRP ≤ ₹1,000 attract 5% GST, and garments with MRP > ₹1,000 attract 12% GST. VeriStock Pro automatically applies the correct rate based on the product's MRP during billing and generates compliant GST invoices.
Yes. VeriStock Pro supports multi-tier pricing — you can set separate retail price, wholesale price, and dealer price for each product. When billing a wholesale customer, select the wholesale price tier and all items are billed at wholesale rates automatically.
