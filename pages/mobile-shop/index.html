Mobile Shop FAQ

Common questions from mobile shop owners using VeriStock Pro.

VeriStock Pro records both IMEI 1 and IMEI 2 for every device during purchase, sale, or repair intake. You can search any IMEI instantly to view its complete lifecycle history — purchase date, sale date, buyer details, and all associated repair records. IMEI data is stored encrypted locally on your device.
Under GST Rule 32(5), tax on second-hand devices is computed only on the profit margin (Selling Price minus Purchase Price), not on the full sale value. VeriStock Pro automatically calculates this during billing when the "Margin Scheme" option is selected, ensuring you pay the correct GST without overpaying.
The Digital KYC captures: seller name, Aadhaar card number, photo ID (camera-captured on the spot), device IMEI number, FMI lock status, device physical condition notes, purchase price, and GPS geolocation. The completed KYC generates a legally compliant buyback receipt that can be printed via Bluetooth thermal printer.
